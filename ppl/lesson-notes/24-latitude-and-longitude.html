Lesson 24 — Latitude and Longitude

▶ Watch lessonBack to course

Core idea

  • Latitude and longitude form a global coordinate grid for exact positions.
  • Latitude measures north/south of the equator; longitude measures east/west of the prime meridian.

Latitude

  • Equator = 0°; poles = 90° N/S.
  • Lines of latitude run east-west.
  • 1° latitude ≈ 60 NM; 1 minute latitude ≈ 1 NM.

Longitude

  • Prime meridian = 0°; longitude extends to 180° E/W.
  • Meridians converge toward the poles, so a minute of longitude is not always 1 NM.

Coordinate formats and GPS

  • 60 minutes = 1 degree; 60 seconds = 1 minute.
  • Coordinates may be degrees-minutes-seconds, degrees/decimal-minutes, or decimal degrees.
  • Verify format and N/S/E/W before entering a GPS waypoint; a small transcription error can move the point many miles.

What to remember

  • Latitude N/S; longitude E/W.
  • 1 minute latitude ≈ 1 NM.
  • Verify coordinate format and hemisphere before GPS entry.
