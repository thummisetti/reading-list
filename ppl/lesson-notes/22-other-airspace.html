Core idea

  • Not every important airspace area is a Class or SUA. FAA's 'other airspace' includes TFRs, military training routes, published VFR routes, TRSAs, SFRAs, parachute areas, and advisory/service areas.
  • These areas can materially affect route planning even when the underlying Class remains unchanged.

Temporary Flight Restrictions

  • TFRs temporarily restrict operations for hazards, disasters, security/VIP movement, major events, and other reasons.
  • TFR details are issued by NOTAM and specify lateral/vertical limits, effective times, and permitted operations.
  • Pilots must check NOTAMs before flight; a sectional alone cannot show current TFRs.

Military Training Routes

  • MTRs support high-speed military training.
  • IR routes are conducted under IFR; VR routes under VFR.
  • Four-digit route numbers generally indicate segments at/below 1,500 AGL; three-digit routes may include operations above 1,500 AGL.

TRSA

  • Terminal Radar Service Areas provide optional radar services around certain terminals.
  • TRSA participation is encouraged but the underlying airspace classification remains what the chart shows.

SFRA/SATR and DC area

  • Special Flight Rules Areas impose special procedures that can include training, flight-plan, communication, transponder, routing, or authorization requirements.
  • The Washington, DC SFRA/FRZ is a high-consequence example; use current procedures and NOTAMs.

Published VFR routes / parachute activity

  • VFR flyways, corridors, transition routes, and other published routes help pilots navigate busy terminal areas.
  • Parachute jump areas and advisory information should be considered during route planning and traffic scanning.

What to remember

  • Check NOTAMs for TFRs every flight.
  • Know MTR numbering concept.
  • TRSA service is optional but useful.
  • Special flight-rule areas can impose unique requirements beyond the underlying class.

Study aid only. Use current FAA publications, your aircraft POH/AFM, and flight instruction for authoritative guidance.
