Lesson 44 — Flight Service Stations

▶ Watch lesson   Back to course

Core idea

  • Flight Service provides pilot weather briefings, flight-plan services, NOTAM information, and other preflight/inflight assistance.
  • A briefing is a decision-support input; the pilot in command remains responsible for the go/no-go decision.

Briefing types

  • Standard briefing: full briefing when you have not already received a complete briefing.
  • Abbreviated briefing: update/specific information when you already know much of the picture.
  • Outlook briefing: typically when departure is well in the future; closer to departure, obtain current information.

What a briefing covers

  • Adverse conditions, synopsis, current conditions, en-route and destination forecasts, winds aloft, NOTAMs, and other pertinent items.
  • Give route, altitude, aircraft type, departure/ETA, and pilot needs so the briefing is relevant.

Inflight use

  • Flight Service/ATC can provide weather updates and accept PIREPs depending on service/coverage.
  • Use onboard weather as supplemental information and understand latency/limitations.

What to remember

  • Standard / abbreviated / outlook.
  • Briefing supports, but does not make, the PIC decision.
  • Update weather and NOTAMs as departure approaches and conditions change.
