Lesson 54 — Privileges & Limitations

▶ Watch lesson   Back to course

Core idea

  • A private pilot may act as PIC and carry passengers within the ratings, endorsements, currency, medical, aircraft, and operating-rule limits that apply.
  • The major limitation is that a private pilot generally may not act as PIC for compensation or hire, subject to specific exceptions in §61.113.

Passenger carrying / currency

  • To carry passengers, meet recent takeoff-and-landing experience requirements; night passenger carrying has additional night landing currency.
  • A flight review or qualifying alternative is required on the applicable cycle to act as PIC.
  • Complex, high-performance, tailwheel, pressurized/high-altitude, and other operations may require endorsements or ratings.

Expense sharing

  • A private pilot may share only the allowed operating expenses under the rule, and may not pay less than the pilot's pro-rata share.
  • The expenses are limited to the categories allowed by §61.113(c), and the pilot/passengers must have a bona fide common purpose.
  • Holding out transportation to the public can turn a seemingly private flight into an illegal commercial operation.

Permitted exceptions

  • §61.113 contains specific exceptions for certain incidental-business flights, charitable/community-event operations, search-and-location reimbursement, aircraft sales demonstrations by qualifying salespersons, glider/ultralight towing, and other specified circumstances.
  • Do not generalize an exception; verify its exact conditions before relying on it.

Ratings and limitations

  • A certificate grade does not automatically authorize every category/class/type or instrument operation.
  • Operate only within certificate ratings, limitations, endorsements, medical qualification, and aircraft limitations.

What to remember

  • Private pilot = passengers and limited business use, but generally no compensation/hire.
  • Expense sharing requires pro-rata share + allowed expenses + common purpose.
  • Currency, endorsements, ratings, medical qualification, and aircraft limitations all still apply.
