Lesson 33 — The Magnetic Compass

▶ Watch lesson   Back to course

Core idea

  • The magnetic compass aligns with Earth’s magnetic field and provides a simple heading reference independent of most aircraft electrical/vacuum systems.
  • Its predictable turning and acceleration errors must be understood.

Variation and deviation

  • Variation = angle between true north and magnetic north.
  • Deviation = compass error caused by aircraft magnetic/electrical influences; residual error appears on the compass correction card.

Northern Hemisphere turning error

  • UNOS: Undershoot North, Overshoot South when rolling out using the compass.
  • Error is greatest near north/south and smaller near east/west.

Acceleration error

  • ANDS: Accelerate North, Decelerate South on east/west headings in the Northern Hemisphere.
  • Read the compass in straight-and-level, unaccelerated flight when possible.

What to remember

  • Variation ≠ deviation.
  • UNOS; ANDS (Northern Hemisphere).
  • Use compass to set/check the heading indicator.
