Lesson 30 — The Turn Coordinator

▶ Watch lesson   Back to course

Core idea

  • The turn coordinator shows rate of turn and initial roll tendency; the ball/inclinometer shows slip/skid coordination.
  • It does not directly display bank angle like an attitude indicator.

Standard rate

  • Standard-rate turn = 3° per second, completing 360° in 2 minutes.
  • Aligning the miniature airplane with the standard-rate mark indicates turn rate, not a fixed bank angle.

Ball / coordination

  • Ball centered = coordinated flight.
  • Ball inside = slip; outside = skid in the usual interpretation.
  • Use rudder toward the ball while maintaining desired bank/path: “step on the ball.”

Power and redundancy

  • Many turn coordinators are electrically powered, providing redundancy in older vacuum-instrument panels.
  • Know whether your airplane instead has a turn-and-slip indicator and how it differs.

What to remember

  • 3°/sec = standard rate.
  • Miniature airplane = rate/tendency, not true bank.
  • Ball shows coordination.
