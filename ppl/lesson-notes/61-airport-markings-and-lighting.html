Lesson 61 — Airport Markings & Lighting

▶ Watch lesson   Back to course

Core idea

  • Airport surface colors are highly standardized: runway markings are white; taxiway markings are yellow.
  • Signs, markings, and lights tell you where you are, where you may go, and where you must stop.

Runway markings

  • Runway designators reflect magnetic direction rounded to the nearest 10° with the final zero omitted; parallel runways add L/C/R.
  • Threshold, centerline, aiming-point, touchdown-zone, and side-stripe markings depend on runway type.
  • Displaced threshold arrows/arrowheads define pavement not available for landing at that end but potentially usable for taxi/takeoff/landing rollout as published.

Taxiway / hold-short

  • Taxiway centerline and edge markings are yellow.
  • Runway holding-position marking: two solid + two dashed yellow lines. From taxiway side, stop before the solid lines unless cleared/authorized to cross.
  • ILS critical-area and other holding markings have distinct patterns; obey ATC/signage.

Signs

  • Red background / white characters: mandatory instruction—runway holding position, ILS critical area, no-entry.
  • Black background / yellow characters: location—where you are.
  • Yellow background / black characters: direction/destination/information.

Lighting

  • Runway edge lights are generally white, transitioning to yellow near the far end on instrument runways; runway end/threshold lights use red/green depending on viewing direction.
  • Taxiway edge lights are blue; taxiway centerline lights are green where installed.
  • REILs identify runway ends with synchronized flashing lights.
  • PAPI/VASI provide visual glidepath information; know the red/white indication for your system.
  • Pilot-controlled lighting can be activated by radio microphone clicks on the published frequency where installed.

Worth watching

  • This is a strongly visual lesson. Use the video/FAA airport-signs-and-markings graphics at least once to build pattern recognition; use these notes for later recall.

What to remember

  • Runway white; taxiway yellow.
  • Red sign = mandatory; black = location; yellow = direction/information.
  • Hold short before the solid pair.
  • Blue taxiway edges; green taxiway centerline.
